Fast, Private, Client-Side Audio Conversion

AudioPipe is an in-browser audio converter and editor powered by WebAssembly (FFmpeg.wasm). Convert WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG, and MP4 video clips into clean MP3 audio files directly on your computer or phone without uploading your private media files to external cloud servers.

Why Use AudioPipe?

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: Audio files are processed locally on your device's CPU. Your media never leaves your browser.
  • No File Size Restrictions: Process full-length podcasts, studio recordings, and video clips without running into 25MB file limits.
  • Precision Trimming: Extract exact audio segments, intros, or sound bites by setting start and end timestamps.

How to Convert Audio Files

  1. Select any audio or video file from your computer or smartphone.
  2. Choose your target audio format (MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A).
  3. Optionally enter start and end timestamps to trim the audio.
  4. Click "Convert & Process Audio" to generate your instant download link.
